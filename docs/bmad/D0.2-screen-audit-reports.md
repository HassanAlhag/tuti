# D0.2 — Screen Audit Reports
## Design Audit Records · Customer Web · v1.0

> Each audit entry in this document is produced by applying the
> [D0.1 Design Quality Checklist](D0.1-design-quality-checklist.md)
> to a specific screen. No screen may be marked PASS without completing
> all eight steps of the D0.1 audit workflow, including the Human Taste
> Review (Section 7 of D0.1).
>
> Audits are ordered by the priority queue defined in D0.1 Section 10.
> Complete audits in priority order; do not skip ahead.

---

## How to Use This Document

1. Pick the next unaudited screen from the priority queue (lowest priority number
   with status other than PASS).
2. Open [D0.1](D0.1-design-quality-checklist.md) and work through all eight steps.
3. Copy the **Scoring Template** from D0.1 Section 9 into the matching placeholder
   section below.
4. Fill in all fields: scores, hard/soft fails, taste notes, decision, remediations.
5. Update the **Audit Index** table at the top of this document.
6. A screen may have multiple audit entries over time (early audit → remediation →
   final audit). Add new entries below the previous one; do not overwrite.

### Stage definitions

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Early** | First-pass audit against design mockup or early implementation |
| **Final** | Audit against the shipped or near-shipped implementation; all remediations resolved |

### Status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | Not yet audited |
| `in-progress` | Audit started; scoring incomplete |
| `conditional` | Early audit passed (all ≥ 3, no hard fails); remediations outstanding |
| `pass` | Final audit passed (all ≥ 4, no hard fails, taste review done) |
| `fail` | Audit failed; cannot proceed or ship |

#### Step 7 — Human taste review

*Initial review conducted at early audit (2026-06-18). Final verdicts confirmed after Fix Batches A, B, and C.*

**Premium feel: PASS**
The composition is unmistakably premium — dark forest stage, commanding clamp headline, controlled gold, 480ms crossfade with `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)` entrance. The `.is-invite` fix is exactly right: it feels like a whisper, not a shout. The chapter nav with thin gold active border is elegant wayfinding. The progress bar in warm white is nearly invisible at rest — more elegant than the previous gold version.

**Warm and giftable: PASS**
The chapter copy is warm and personal throughout. "Begin with a fragrance that suits the mood, the moment, and the person you have in mind." The narrative arc builds from choosing a scent to gifting it beautifully — emotionally correct for a luxury gifting brand. The invite "Build your gift →" uses the right word ("gift", not "shop" or "buy"). Alt text confirms the imagery tells a physical gift-building story across four chapters.

**Durable and scalable: PASS**
Architecturally solid after three Fix Batches. Chapter count decoupled from CSS. Token system used throughout. Mobile and reduced-motion handled at the hook level. SSR-safe. No layout that breaks at edge content lengths. Adding a fifth chapter requires one data entry and nothing else.

**Gold usage restrained: PASS**
Two gold elements at rest: kicker text (chapter position signal) and active nav border (wayfinding accent). Both are thin, purposeful, at moments of premium signal. Progress bar is warm white. No gold fills. No gold on buttons. Fully compliant with D0.1 max 2 per screen — and both uses are exemplary rather than marginal.

**Chapter 1 invite subtle, not salesy: PASS**
`rgba(255,255,255,0.72)` white, 0.78rem uppercase, whisper bottom-border on hover only. No fill. A 13px `<ArrowRight>` inline. It reads as an editorial pointer — an invitation from the brand, not a conversion mechanic. It would not read as intrusive or promotional to a luxury consumer.

**Overall taste verdict: PASS.** The screen is ready to be seen by a high-value customer. Three Fix Batches raised quality without changing the design language. The chapter 1 invite is appropriately restrained. The gold is controlled. The typography has correct minimum sizing. The accessibility is comprehensive. The D3 = 3 score reflects a design philosophy choice (full-bleed photography demands no competing glass surfaces), not a visible quality gap.
